# Privacy Policy


NAMINOL LLC ("we") handles your information in the diary app "Seed Diary" (the "App") as described in this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

- **Account information**: the identifier issued when you sign in with Apple or Google, and your email address if one is provided
- **Age check result**: we only store a flag that you confirmed you are 13 or older, and a flag for whether you are 18 or older. We do not store the birth year and month you enter
- **Diary**: entry text, dates, and attached photos
- **Your companion and family line**: names, pixel-art images, form records (species names and explanations of why it took that form and how it grew, which may briefly quote your diary), backgrounds, feelings status, theme songs, and encyclopedia records
- **Internal notes made from your diary**: classifications made when your diary is read overnight, and short memories (one sentence each about events and feelings) that your companion uses in conversation. These are not shown to you
- **Friends**: friendships, invitations, visits, blocks, and reports
- **Device settings**: display language, time zone, region (a two-letter country code used for seasons, the order of support services, and ad settings), push notification tokens, and notification settings
- **Time of the last support suggestion**: we only store when we last suggested support services. We do not store what the suggestion was about
- **Purchases**: the items you bought and records of verifying the purchase. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never receive your card details
- **Advertising**: records of ads shown and rewarded videos completed. Ad providers may collect your device's advertising identifier (see Section 5)

Voice answers are converted to text by your device's speech recognition. We do not send or store your voice on our servers (your device's operating system provider may process speech recognition).

## 2. How we use information

- To create your companion's forms, backgrounds, and theme song from your diary
- To write your companion's short replies, morning messages, conversations, and full stories
- To gently suggest support services once (at most once every 7 days) when you write about wanting to end your life or wanting to kill someone
- To provide friends, notifications, purchases, and ads
- To prevent misuse and respond to inquiries

## 3. Your diary

- Your diary and photos are stored so that only you can read them. Friends and other users never see your diary
- Friends can see only your companion's form, name, generation, family tree, and visits. You can also hide its current form
- Shared images and videos contain only your companion's form, name, generation, the App name, a store link (and the chorus of the theme song). They never contain your diary text
- We do not read your diary except when necessary, such as to fix a problem. We do not record diary text in logs

## 4. AI and service providers

The App sends your diary text and conversations to the Claude API provided by Anthropic, PBC.

- **When you save an entry**: we send that entry to create your companion's short reply and to check whether to suggest support services. The result of that check is not stored
- **Overnight reading**: we send your diary text up to that day to create the classifications reflected in your companion's form, the next morning's message, and short memories
- **Form records and song lyrics**: we send your diary text and previous records to create species names, explanations, and lyrics
- **Conversations and talk-to-write**: we send what you say in the conversation and short memories from the past two weeks
- We never attach identifiers such as your account ID or email address (anything you write in your diary is sent as written)
- According to Anthropic's official documentation, content sent through commercial products such as the API is not used to train AI models by default. Inputs and outputs are generally deleted from Anthropic's systems within 30 days of receipt or generation. However, content flagged by automated systems as violating Anthropic's usage policy may be kept for up to 2 years (classification scores for up to 7 years), and content may be kept longer where the law requires (confirmed on October 27, 2026)

We use the following providers to store, process, and deliver data.

**Transfers to third parties in foreign countries (Article 28 of Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information)**: the providers above are third parties outside Japan. We entrust data to them after confirming that they have systems that meet the standards set by the rules of Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission (that is, measures to handle personal information appropriately under contracts or terms of service). We check changes to their terms and practices so that those measures continue. You can learn about the personal information protection systems of the United States and Singapore in the Commission's published survey of foreign personal information protection systems. On request through the contact in Section 11, we will promptly tell you about the measures we take

## 5. Advertising

- The App shows ads through Google AdMob. In the UK, the EEA, and similar regions, we ask for your consent before ads (Google's consent screen)
- Personalized ads use your device's advertising identifier and similar data. You can limit the use of the advertising identifier in your device settings
- We never use your diary text, conversations, or feelings status for advertising
- We do not show ads while you write your diary, during conversations, on the support services screen, or right after you open the App
- Users under 18 are shown only non-personalized ads

## 6. Retention

- Diary, companion, and family line data are kept until you delete your account
- Server logs (which never contain diary text) are deleted within 90 days
- Usage records of AI, image, and song processing (which never contain diary text) are kept after you delete your account, in a form no longer linked to you
- Purchase records in the App's database are deleted when you delete your account. Transaction records that the law requires us to keep for accounting and tax purposes are kept for the period set by law (7 years)
- Backups are deleted within 30 days of being made

## 7. Deleting your account

- You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account
- Deleting your account permanently deletes all your data, including your account, diary, photos, companion and family line, theme songs, friendships, conversations, and notification settings
- We may keep purchase records only for as long as the law requires
- After you delete your account, your data is also fully removed from backups within 30 days

## 8. Children under 13

The App is for people aged 13 and older. Children under 13 may not use it. We check age when you first start, and children under 13 cannot continue. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly (including under the US COPPA).

## 9. Your rights by region

### United Kingdom and European Economic Area (UK GDPR / GDPR)

- **Legal bases**: providing the App (performance of a contract), ads and analytics (consent or legitimate interests), and legal obligations
- Your diary may contain special category data, such as information about your mental health. We process it only to provide the App's features from the diary you write and send (explicit consent)
- You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object, data portability, and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to a supervisory authority (the ICO in the UK)
- When we transfer data outside the UK or EEA, we rely on the safeguards required by law that each provider offers, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (SCCs), the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or the UK Addendum to the SCCs, and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework
- **Users under 18 (UK Age Appropriate Design Code)**: settings are high-privacy by default. Your diary is visible only to you, we do not collect location, and friends can connect only by invitation. Only non-personalized ads are shown

### California, USA (CCPA / CPRA)

- The categories of information we collect are in Section 1, the purposes in Section 2, and the recipients in Section 4
- We do not sell your personal information for money
- The use of your device's advertising identifier and similar data by our ad provider (Google AdMob) for personalized ads may count as "sharing" (sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising) under the CCPA. You can opt out ("Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information") as follows
  - iOS: the App does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), so your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not passed to ad providers. You can also stop apps from asking to track you in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  - Android: you can delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (the wording differs by device)
  - Email support@naminol.com with "Do Not Sell or Share", and we will handle your request
  - The App does not have an in-app setting for this
- You have the right to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale and sharing, and limit the use of sensitive personal information, and you will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

### Canada, Australia, and New Zealand

Under Canada's PIPEDA, Australia's Privacy Act 1988, and New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020, you may request access to and correction of your information, and you may complain to the privacy regulator in your country.

### Japan

You may request disclosure, correction, suspension of use, or deletion of your retained personal data under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information.

## 10. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections, row-level access control in our database, and private storage of photos, images, and songs.
